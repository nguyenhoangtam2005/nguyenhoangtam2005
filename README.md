### UX/UI Designer | Product Thinking | Frontend Foundation

I'm a UX/UI Designer focused on creating **user-centered, practical, and scalable digital experiences**.

My work combines **UX thinking, product understanding, and interface design** — from understanding user problems and structuring information to designing clear interfaces and refining usability.

I care about more than how a product looks. I focus on **why a feature exists, how users interact with it, and how the experience can be simplified and improved.**

With a foundation in frontend development, I also understand technical constraints and implementation feasibility, allowing me to collaborate more effectively with developers and design interfaces that are realistic to build.

## 🧠 Design Approach

I believe good design starts with **understanding the problem before designing the solution**.

My approach:

**Discover → Define → Structure → Design → Test → Refine**

I continuously ask:

* What problem are we solving?
* Why does this feature exist?
* How will users interact with it?
* Can the experience be simpler?
* Is the solution practical to implement?

---

## 💻 Frontend Foundation

Frontend development is a supporting skill that helps me **bridge the gap between design and implementation**.

It allows me to better understand technical constraints, evaluate implementation feasibility, communicate effectively with developers, and create interfaces that are practical to build.

---

## 🚀 Currently

I'm continuing to deepen my **UX/UI expertise, product thinking, and design system knowledge**, while strengthening my frontend foundation.

My goal is to bridge the gap between **user needs, product requirements, design decisions, and technical implementation** — creating products that are clear, usable, practical, and buildable.

---

### 💬 Design Philosophy

> **Good design is not about adding more.
> It's about making the right things clear, useful, and easy to use.**
